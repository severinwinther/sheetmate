Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SheetMate is developed by Severin Kokaas Winther. The app does not collect, store or share personal data.

  • No account and no tracking. The app has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Your projects. Boards, parts, projects and offcuts are stored on your device. With SheetMate Pro they also sync through your own iCloud account. That storage is run by Apple, and the developer has no access to it.
  • Purchases. SheetMate Pro is sold through the App Store and handled by Apple. The developer receives no payment or account details.
  • Export. PDF and CSV files are created on your device and shared only where you choose.
  • Deletion. Delete projects in the app, or remove the app, to delete your data. iCloud data can be removed under Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
