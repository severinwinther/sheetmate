Frequently asked questions

How do I make a cutting plan?

On the Setup tab, set kerf, edge margin and part spacing. Add the boards you have with + Add under Boards, and the pieces you need under Parts. Then tap Optimise Cutting Layout. The result shows how many boards you need, the layout of each board and a complete cut list.

What is the difference between kerf, edge margin and part spacing?
  • Kerf is the thickness of the saw blade or cutter. It is included in the edge margin, not added on top of it.
  • Edge margin is the distance from the parts to the edge of the board.
  • Part spacing is the distance between parts, for the cut itself and for CNC hold-down.
How does grain direction work?

Boards and parts can both be marked as having grain. A part with grain on a board with grain keeps its direction and is never rotated. Turn on Override grain — all parts to let every part rotate when that saves material.

Can I keep parts together, for example doors with continuous grain?

Yes. Edit a part and choose Link to another part. A part can be linked to up to two others by name, and you choose which edge they meet on. Linked parts are placed edge to edge on the same board, with the part spacing between them.

How is edge banding handled?

Choose which edges of a part get banding. Under Edge banding on the Setup tab you set the banding thickness, which is subtracted from the cut size so the finished part matches your measurements, and an extra length per edge. The result shows how many metres of banding you need.

How do I reuse offcuts?

After optimising, tap Save offcuts on the Results tab to keep the largest leftover from each board. In your next project, open Offcut stock on the Setup tab and add the pieces you want to use. Offcuts are used before full boards.

How do I export the plan?

On the Results tab, tap Export and choose the cutting plan as PDF (board diagrams and full cut list), the cut list as CSV (for Excel, Numbers or CNC programming), or both.

What is the difference between Fast and Advanced?

Fast gives a good plan in a moment. Advanced searches far more layouts to use as few boards as possible, and takes a little longer. Advanced is part of SheetMate Pro.

What is SheetMate Pro?

SheetMate Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription. It unlocks Advanced optimisation and syncs your projects and offcuts between your devices through your own iCloud. Everything else in SheetMate is free.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore it?

Open the Projects tab, tap SheetMate Pro and then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

My projects don't show up on my other device

iCloud sync needs SheetMate Pro and the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on on both devices. Syncing can take a minute or two. Your projects are always kept on each device as well, so nothing is lost while waiting.

Do I need an account?

No. SheetMate has no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

What do I need to run SheetMate?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
